Apple vs. Microsoft Financial Performance Analysis

Financial Statement Analysis · Excel Modeling · Ratio Analysis

01 · Project Overview

How do two of the world's largest technology companies compare in profitability, efficiency, and financial stability? My partner and I analyzed Apple and Microsoft's 2020–2022 financial statements to explore how their different business models translate into financial performance.

02 · Approach & Analysis

Using the companies' annual 10-K filings, we organized financial statement data in a spreadsheet and performed common-size analysis of income statements and balance sheets. We calculated and compared gross profit margin, net profit margin, earnings per share, total asset turnover, and current ratios across three fiscal years. We also explored how net profit margin, asset turnover, and financial leverage contribute to return on assets (ROA) and return on equity (ROE).

03 · Key Findings

  • Growth: Both companies increased revenue and net income from 2020 to 2022. Apple recorded faster revenue growth over the period, while Microsoft's net profit margin remained higher.
  • Operating efficiency: Apple's 2022 asset turnover of 1.12 was nearly twice Microsoft's 0.57, suggesting Apple generated more revenue per dollar of assets.
  • Liquidity: Microsoft's 2022 current ratio was 1.78, compared with Apple's 0.88, indicating a stronger short-term liquidity position by this measure.
  • Investment perspective: We considered how profitability, asset efficiency, leverage, and broader market risks inform a financial assessment rather than relying on a single ratio.

All figures refer to the 2020–2022 period examined in this Fall 2023 academic project; they are not current financial metrics or investment advice.

05 · Reflection

This project helped me move beyond reading individual financial figures to understanding the stories they tell together. Comparing Apple and Microsoft showed me why higher profit margins do not necessarily mean greater asset efficiency, and how liquidity, leverage, and business strategy shape a company's financial position. Working with real 10-K filings also strengthened my spreadsheet modeling and analytical communication skills, especially when translating financial ratios into conclusions someone could use to evaluate a business.
